03 / failure and repair are different sets

Keep a conflict.
Remove a repair.

Confirmed inclusion-minimal conflicts

Each conflict fails, and deleting any one of its equations succeeds. These immediate checks suffice because any subset of a unifiable finite-term system is also unifiable.

Confirmed inclusion-minimal deletion repairs

A repair lists indices to REMOVE from the original system. The remainder unifies, and restoring any removed equation fails. Empty deletion is the sole minimal repair when the full system unifies. A limit in a required neighbor prevents confirmation.
